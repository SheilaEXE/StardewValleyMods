# Stay Hydrated!

This mod adds a glass and a bottle of ice-cold water to the game. Drink some water to restore energy and gain a temporary Freshness buff that helps you spend less energy while using tools.

## Freshness Buff

Glass of Iced Water: +1 Freshness for 2 minutes and 5% less energy spent when using tools.
Bottle of Iced Water: +2 Freshness for 4 minutes and 10% less energy spent when using tools.

## How to Get Water

Buy bottles from Pierre or Gus for 100g each.
Collect a free glass of water from sinks using a configurable keyboard, mouse, or controller button.

## Weather Wonders Integration

If Weather Wonders is installed, drinking water during a heatwave protects you from the intense heat while the Freshness buff is active.
It prevents both the maximum-energy penalty and the continuous energy drain caused by the heat. Both drinks provide full protection, the bottle simply lasts longer.

## Requirements
- SMAPI
- Content Patcher
- Tile Marker 1.1.0 or later (Required for getting water from the sink.)
- Generic Mod Config Menu (To configure the sink interaction button in-game)
